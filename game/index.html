EMBER DUEL

A tactical card battler of flame, tide, leaf & stone

❤️ Reduce the Cinder Warden from 30 life to 0 to win.
🔷 Mana grows each turn. Cards cost mana to play.
⚔️ Fighters attack once a turn. Flame beats Leaf, Leaf beats Stone, Stone beats Tide, Tide beats Flame — advantage deals +2.
🛡️ Guards must be attacked first. Swift fighters strike the turn they arrive.

Mouse + keyboard · 1–5 play cards · A attack · N end turn · H help

How to play Ember Duel

  • Goal: bring the Cinder Warden's life from 30 to 0 before yours reaches 0.
  • Mana: you gain 1 mana crystal each turn (up to 10). Playing a card spends its cost.
  • Fighters: cards stay on the table and attack once per turn. A fighter cannot attack the turn it is played, unless it is Swift.
  • Guard: while the enemy has a Guard fighter, you must attack a Guard first.
  • Elements: 🔥 Flame beats 🍃 Leaf, 🍃 Leaf beats 🪨 Stone, 🪨 Stone beats 💧 Tide, 💧 Tide beats 🔥 Flame. Attacking a weaker element deals +2 damage.
  • Traits: Ember — deals 1 damage when played. Heal — restores life when played.
  • Draw: you draw a card each turn. An empty deck deals 1 fatigue damage per draw.
  • Controls: mouse — click a hand card to play it, click your fighter then a target to attack. Keyboard — 1–5 select a hand card, Enter plays it, A arms an attacker, Tab cycles targets, N ends turn, M sound, H help, Esc cancels.

VICTORY!

The Cinder Warden falls. The embers are yours.
